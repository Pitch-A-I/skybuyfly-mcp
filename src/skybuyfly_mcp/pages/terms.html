Terms of use for SkyBuyFly in AI assistants

These terms cover the SkyBuyFly MCP server, which ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI and other AI assistants use to search SkyBuyFly's duty-free price comparison. The server's address is:

https://skybuyfly.pitchai.net/mcp

Last updated 9 November 2026

In short: the service is free and read-only. SkyBuyFly compares prices; it does not sell anything or take orders. A price can change at any time, and the airport shop's own price and terms at checkout apply.

Who provides the service

SkyBuyFly and this MCP server are operated by PitchAI. Contact: info@pitchai.net or the support page.

What the service does

  • The server is free to use, needs no account and offers read-only tools: it searches products, shows prices per airport shop, price history, airports and currencies, and can check a price live on the shop's own page.
  • SkyBuyFly does not sell products, take orders, reserve items or handle payments. Links open SkyBuyFly's product and offer pages, which hand off to the airport shop's own website.
  • Tobacco and nicotine products are not available through the service.

Prices and availability

  • Prices are taken from the airport shops' own websites. A stored price shows the date it was observed and can be out of date; a live-confirmed price was read from the shop's page at the time shown.
  • Prices, availability and offers can change at any time. The price and terms of the shop where you buy apply, including any conditions for collection at the airport, travel destination or purchase limits.
  • Prices in another currency than the shop's are converted for comparison only; the shop charges in its own currency.

Fair use and rate limits

Use the service for personal price comparison through an AI assistant or MCP client. Do not use it to copy the catalogue in bulk, to overload it or to get around its limits. The server limits the number of tool calls and live price checks per minute; when a limit is reached, it answers with a message to try again later. We may block traffic that harms the service or other users.

No warranty

The service is provided as it is and as available, without warranty of any kind. We do not guarantee that prices, availability or other information are complete, correct or current, or that the service is always available. The service can change or stop at any time.

Other terms and privacy

  • What the server receives and logs is described in the privacy notice for SkyBuyFly in AI assistants.
  • Your AI service (for example OpenAI for ChatGPT or Anthropic for Claude) applies its own terms to your conversation.
