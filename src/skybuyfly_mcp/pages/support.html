Is the service up?

The server's status is at:

https://skybuyfly.pitchai.net/mcp/health

It answers "status": "ok" while the server runs. How to connect each assistant is described on SkyBuyFly for AI assistants.

Common questions

  • The connection fails. Use the server address above with the Streamable HTTP transport and no authentication.
  • "Too many requests" or "live checks are busy". The server limits tool calls and live price checks per minute. Wait the number of seconds the message names and try again; stored prices stay available through get_product.
  • A price differs from the shop. Stored prices carry the date they were observed and can be out of date. Ask for a live check, or open the offer's link to see the shop's current price. The shop's own price applies.
  • A product is missing. SkyBuyFly does not cover every airport, shop or product, and tobacco and nicotine products are not available.

What to include in a report

  • The assistant or client you used (for example ChatGPT, Claude on the web, Claude Code or Cursor).
  • The tool, if you know it, and what you asked: the product, barcode, airports and currency.
  • The date and time of the request, with your time zone.
  • The answer or error message you got, and what you expected instead.

Please do not include personal details that are not needed to look into the problem.
